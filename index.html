Development pipeline (all projects)

Indigo = residential (sized by units), amber = commercial (sized by sq ft); solid = under construction, ring = announced, faint = conceptual. Commercial pipeline & tenants (TABS)
State-registered commercial construction across 9 DFW counties: new buildings and retail shells (future leasable space) with estimated delivery dates, committed tenants by center, and a veterinary early-warning feed — clinics visible in filings months before opening (red paw pins; green = confirmed open via state sales-tax registration). Public records (TDLR TABS + Comptroller). Dates are applicant estimates.

Off-market land (vacant commercial)
Vacant, commercially-viable parcels across all 9 DFW counties with the owner of record and mailing address from county appraisal rolls — the off-market call list. Solid green = county-classified vacant commercial; teal = vacant land on a mapped retail corridor; gray = larger tract near retail. Dot size = acreage. Public record, not listings. Sources: CCAD / DCAD / DentonCAD / TAD + TxGIO StratMap 2025.

Developing communities (master-planned)
35 major DFW master-planned communities under development, with resident and pet-household estimates at buildout. Real mapped boundaries where available (20 of 35); circles sized by announced homes elsewhere. Bold teal = in the pad-engagement window (approaching the 1,500–2,500-rooftop trigger or anchor committed — when chains sign sites, 12–24 months before opening). Dashed = announced. Combinable with any fill layer.
